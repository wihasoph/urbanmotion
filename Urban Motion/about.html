Pioneering urban mobility

Urban Motion is a eco-friendly electric scooter and e-bike sharing network. We provide a convenient alternative to cars, reducing CO₂ emissions and cutting down city traffic congestion.

Our Story

Our core priorities

Sustainability

Our entire fleet operates on 100% clean electric power, preventing harmful emissions across urban spaces.

Safety first

Automatic speed reduction in pedestrian zones and routine technical inspections for every vehicle.

Accessibility

Strategically placed parking zones near transit hubs, universities, and business districts for maximum convenience.

Milestones and progress

2024

Startup launch

Initial pilot launch in the city center with a fleet of 50 electric scooters and a dedicated founding team.

2025

Fleet expansion

Extended coverage to five new districts and introduced the City Bike E-1 electric bicycle line.

2026

Sustainable growth

Transitioned to fully automated charging hubs and surpassed over 1,000,000 completed rides.

Riding guidelines

  • Dismount at Crosswalks: Always walk your vehicle across pedestrian crossings.
  • Single Rider Rule: Riding with passengers is prohibited.
  • Park Responsibly: Do not block sidewalks, ramps, or doors.
